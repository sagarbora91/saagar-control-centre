**Target:** payroll.html
**Findings:** 10

**Coverage notes:** Scope covered: read the full payroll.html (3305 lines) CSS + JS render functions, and the shell's payroll MOBILE_CSS block (index.html lines 6309-6339), the uniform-CSS control/text floors (6072-6167), the FAB/back-home injection (6027-6053), and injectSafetyNet (6413-6443). Positives verified (NOT defects): all wide tables live in .table-scroll/.stbl-wrap/inline overflow-x:auto wrappers and scroll; att/ms/gm tables card-reflow at <=520px with full data-label coverage and name-cell/gm-del-cell classes present in JS output; all form grids (minmax 150/170/180/220/240) collapse to 1col via the module's own @media(max-width:640px) even where the shell's minmax list omits 150px, because relevant phone widths (360/412) are <=640; MOBILE_CSS is correctly scoped under html.bcc-mobile[data-mod=payroll] with no desktop leak; bottom-nav shows and tab-nav hides at <900px in BOTH modes (width-based), so the 1280xMobile / 360xDesktop mismatch combos degrade gracefully; sig-check/checkbox controls correctly excluded from the 44px floor.

Needs LIVE browser / on-device confirmation: (1) L01 exact employee count at which the att-import modal footer goes off-screen on a real 1280x800 desktop and in 1280 mobile mode — high confidence from CSS but the tipping row-count is data-dependent. (2) L02/L03 flexbox left-edge clipping is browser-engine dependent; confirm in the Android WebView build that the left margin of the 210mm slip/letter is truly unreachable by horizontal drag. (3) L05/L06 currency clipping/wrapping depends on the actual magnitude of a firm's monthly Net Payable / Total CTC and the device font metrics — reproduce with a seeded high-value payroll. (4) L10 FAB-vs-bottom-nav overlap is a pixel-geometry call best verified on a physical 360px device. No JS logic, storage, or library changes were proposed; all fixes are CSS/format or shell-side (control-floor td relief in L04 is a shell-owner item, out of payroll.html scope).

## [P1] payroll-L01 — Attendance Import diff modal footer (Cancel / Apply) unreachable on wide viewports — no modal max-height
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** high | **Combo:** 1280xDesktop, 1280xMobile
- **Location:** payroll.html — .modal (line 270) + .modal-body (line 273); att-import modal #att-import-modal (line 1329-1337) whose body is filled by renderAttImportModal() (line 3247-3285). Footer buttons built at lines 3281-3284.
- **Defect:** The generic .modal has `overflow:hidden` and NO max-height (line 270). The internal scroll rule `.modal-body{max-height:80vh;overflow:auto}` exists ONLY inside the module's `@media(max-width:640px)` block (line 566). The Attendance-Import diff modal (`max-width:880px`) renders one table row per employee plus the Cancel / 'Apply selected → draft' buttons INSIDE .modal-body. With ~25+ employees the modal grows taller than an 800px viewport; because `.modal-bg` centers it (`align-items:center`, line 268) and `.modal` clips overflow with no body scroll on viewports >640px, the bottom of the diff table and the Apply/Cancel footer are pushed off-screen and cannot be scrolled to. The shell injects no generic modal max-height (verified injectSafetyNet / uniform CSS). Blocks the import-approval flow.
- **Evidence:** .modal{...;overflow:hidden} (line 270, no max-height); .modal-body{padding:18px;...} (line 273, no max-height); `.modal-body{...max-height:80vh;overflow:auto}` gated inside `@media (max-width:640px)` at line 566; footer `<div ...><button ... closeAttImport()>Cancel</button><button ... applyAttImportSel()>Apply selected → draft</button></div>` appended to #att-import-body (lines 3281-3284).
- **Impact:** On desktop (1280xDesktop) and mobile-mode-on-wide (1280xMobile) with a normal-sized workforce, the maker cannot reach the 'Apply selected → draft' button, blocking the DSR/Leave attendance import flow entirely. The <=640px path is safe (body scrolls).
- **Fix hint:** Move the `.modal-body{max-height:80vh;overflow:auto}` rule out of the 640px media query so it applies in all modes/widths, OR add `.modal{max-height:90vh;display:flex;flex-direction:column}` + `.modal-body{overflow:auto;flex:1 1 auto;min-height:0}` unconditionally so the header stays and the body scrolls with the footer always in reach. CSS-only, no handler change.

## [P1] payroll-L02 — Salary-slip preview clips the left edge of the 210mm slip (flex-center + overflow) — content unreachable
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** high | **Combo:** 360xMobile, 412xMobile, 360xDesktop
- **Location:** payroll.html — #pane-slips .slip-preview (line 390); slip appended by slipDoPreview() (line 2579, buildGmSlip → .gmslip width:210mm at line 408).
- **Defect:** `.slip-preview` is `display:flex;flex-direction:column;align-items:center;overflow:auto` (line 390). The previewed slip is a `.gmslip` fixed at `width:210mm` (~793px, line 408). When a flex container centers a child on its cross axis (`align-items:center`) and that child is WIDER than the container, the overflow spills equally left and right, but scroll can only reach the right/end — the LEFT (start) edge is clipped and unreachable by horizontal scroll (well-known flexbox-centering + overflow bug). On a 360/412 phone the left margin of the salary slip (left column of the earnings/deductions table, 'For SAAGAR TRADERS' signature block) is cut off and cannot be scrolled into view.
- **Evidence:** #pane-slips .slip-preview{display:none;flex-direction:column;align-items:center;...overflow:auto;max-height:80vh} (line 390); .gmslip{width:210mm;...} (line 408); wrap.appendChild(s) in slipDoPreview (line 2579).
- **Impact:** Users previewing salary slips on a phone cannot see or scroll to the left portion of the slip, making the on-screen preview partially unusable (the exported PDF is unaffected).
- **Fix hint:** For the horizontally-overflowing preview, replace `align-items:center` with `align-items:flex-start` (or wrap each slip in a `min-width:max-content` inline-block and center via `margin:auto` on a non-flex block, or use `justify-content:safe center`). CSS-only.

## [P1] payroll-L03 — HR-letter preview clips the left edge of the 210mm letter (flex justify-content:center + overflow)
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** high | **Combo:** 360xMobile, 412xMobile, 360xDesktop
- **Location:** payroll.html — #hr-preview-wrap (line 1084); letter appended by hrPreview() (line 2803, buildHrLetterEl → .gmslip width:210mm).
- **Defect:** `#hr-preview-wrap` is inline-styled `display:none;align-items:center;justify-content:center;...overflow:auto;max-height:80vh` (line 1084) and hrPreview() sets `w.style.display='flex'` (line 2803) then appends a `.gmslip` letter fixed at `width:210mm` (~793px). Same flex-centering + overflow clip as L02: on a narrow viewport the letter is centered and wider than the box, so `justify-content:center` pushes the left edge out of the scrollable area and it is unreachable. The HR letter's left text margin is cut off on phones.
- **Evidence:** id="hr-preview-wrap" style="...align-items:center;justify-content:center;...overflow:auto;max-height:80vh" (line 1084); w.style.display="flex" (line 2803); el.className="gmslip" (line 2779) with .gmslip{width:210mm} (line 408).
- **Impact:** HR-letter on-screen preview is partially unreadable on phones — left margin/body text start is clipped and cannot be scrolled to.
- **Fix hint:** Change `justify-content:center` to `justify-content:flex-start` on #hr-preview-wrap (or wrap the letter so centering happens on a block, not a flex item wider than the container). CSS-only.

## [P2] payroll-L04 — 44px control-height floor forces every data-table cell input to 44px, bloating the dense attendance/salary/master registers
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** high | **Combo:** 1280xDesktop, 1280xMobile (and 360/412 for adv/ap/rp tables that do not card-reflow)
- **Location:** payroll.html — td input/select styling (line 206, 1838-1839, 2101-2131); shell control floor injectUniformCSS (index.html lines 6141-6162).
- **Defect:** The shell's global control floor sets `min-height:44px !important` on every button/input/select/textarea (index.html line 6149) and only relieves `min-width:0` for td/th descendants (line 6158-6162) — NOT min-height. Payroll's editable registers put a text/number/date input in nearly every cell (att-table ~10 numeric inputs per row, ms-table ~15 inputs per row, gm-table gross/type/remark inputs). The module styles them at `padding:6px 5px;font-size:11px` (line 206) expecting ~28-30px rows, but the shell forces each to 44px tall. In wide-scroll-table mode (all >520px combos, plus the Advances/Approval/Reports tables which never card-reflow) this roughly doubles every row height, so a 30-employee register is ~1400px instead of ~900px — far more scrolling, and the tabular_nums calc columns look sparse. The payroll MOBILE_CSS `td input{...padding:5px 4px}` (index.html line 6335) does not override the !important min-height so it cannot shrink these.
- **Evidence:** td input,td select,td textarea{...padding:6px 5px;font-size:11px} (line 206); shell floor `min-height:44px !important` (index.html line 6149) with only `html body td input{min-width:0}` relief (index.html lines 6158-6162, no min-height relief).
- **Impact:** Dense payroll data tables become excessively tall on desktop and wide-mobile, adding significant vertical scrolling and reducing how many employees are visible at once; degrades but does not block the core review flow.
- **Fix hint:** Shell-side (out of this file's scope) the intended relief block should also grant `min-height:0` (or a smaller floor) to `td input/select/textarea`; module-side alternative is to add a bcc-mobile-independent per-module rule reducing cell-input min-height. Do NOT edit storage-core/sqlite-store. Flag for shell owner.

## [P2] payroll-L05 — Long ₹ totals clipped in summary cards (.sc overflow:hidden, no wrap/ellipsis)
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** medium | **Combo:** 360xMobile, 412xMobile
- **Location:** payroll.html — .sc (line 156) / .sc-v (line 164); GM summary render (lines 2074-2079: Net Payable, Total Advance, Total CTC).
- **Defect:** Summary cards are forced to a 2-column grid on mobile (MOBILE_CSS `.summary{grid-template-columns:repeat(2,1fr)}`, index.html line 6324), giving each `.sc` ~160px width at 360px. `.sc-v` renders values at `font-size:22px` serif tabular-nums with NO white-space/overflow handling, and `.sc{overflow:hidden}` (line 156). The GM summary shows `₹${fmtMoney(totalCTC,0)}` / `₹${fmtMoney(totalNet,0)}` un-compacted (lines 2077-2079); a firm's monthly Total CTC of ₹12,34,567 or more (>=10 chars) exceeds the ~132px content width and is silently clipped by `overflow:hidden` rather than wrapping — the last digits are cut off, misreporting the payroll total.
- **Evidence:** .sc{...overflow:hidden} (line 156); .sc-v{font-family:var(--font-serif);font-size:22px;...} (line 164, no white-space/ellipsis); `<div class="sc-v">₹${fmtMoney(totalCTC,0)}</div>` (line 2079); MOBILE_CSS `.summary{grid-template-columns:repeat(2,1fr) !important}` (index.html line 6324).
- **Impact:** The most important payroll numbers (Net Payable, Total CTC) can be truncated on phones with no visual cue, so the owner may read a wrong/partial total.
- **Fix hint:** Add on .sc-v (mobile) `font-size:clamp(15px,4.6vw,22px)` or `white-space:nowrap;overflow:hidden;text-overflow:ellipsis` plus a title attribute, or use a compact ₹ formatter (₹12.3L) for the summary/hero stats. CSS-preferred.

## [P2] payroll-L06 — Long ₹ values wrap/overflow the 3-up hero stat tiles on narrow phones
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** medium | **Combo:** 360xMobile, 412xMobile
- **Location:** payroll.html — .hero-stat / .hero-stat-v (lines 138-140); GM hero stats gm-hs-net / gm-hs-ctc (lines 854-855), set at line 1993.
- **Defect:** In mobile the hero stacks and the 3 hero-stat tiles share one row (module @media <=640: `.hero-stat{flex:1 1 0;min-width:0;padding:8px 8px}`, line 484), giving ~108px per tile at 360px (18px value font at <=360, line 682). `gm-hs-net`/`gm-hs-ctc` are set to `"₹"+fmtMoney(t.net,0)` / `"₹"+fmtMoney(t.ctc,0)` with no compaction (line 1993). `.hero-stat-v` has `line-height:1` and no white-space control (line 139), so a value like ₹12,34,567 wraps to two lines inside the ~108px tile, making the three tiles ragged/unequal height and the number awkward to read.
- **Evidence:** .hero-stat-v{font-family:var(--font-serif);font-size:20px;...line-height:1} (line 139); `.hero-stat{flex:1 1 0;min-width:0;padding:8px 8px}` (line 484); `h2.textContent="₹"+fmtMoney(t.net,0)` (line 1993).
- **Impact:** Net Payable / Total CTC in the hero can wrap to two lines and misalign the stat tiles on phones; cosmetic-to-moderate legibility hit on key figures.
- **Fix hint:** Use a compact currency format for hero stats, or reduce `.hero-stat-v` font on mobile and allow the hero-r to wrap tiles to 2 rows (`flex-wrap:wrap` with `flex:1 1 46%`). CSS/format-only.

## [P3] payroll-L07 — Scrollable 9-item bottom-nav does not auto-scroll the active tab into view
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** high | **Combo:** 360xMobile, 412xMobile, 360xDesktop
- **Location:** payroll.html — .bottom-nav (lines 332-337, 556); switchTab() (lines 1805-1814); BOTTOM_TABS 9 items (line 1800).
- **Defect:** The fixed bottom navigation holds 9 buttons at `min-width:60-64px` (~576px total) inside `overflow-x:auto` (line 333), so it scrolls horizontally on a 360px screen — only ~5-6 tabs are visible at once. switchTab() sets `.active` on the bottom-nav button (line 1812) but never scrolls it into view, and the shell's chipCenter() helper only targets `.tab-chips`, not this `.bottom-nav`. Selecting a later tab (e.g. Settings, Reports) via the top tab-nav or programmatically leaves its bottom-nav entry off-screen until the user manually scrolls the bar, so the current location isn't shown.
- **Evidence:** .bottom-nav{...overflow-x:auto;...} (line 333); `.bottom-nav button{...min-width:64px}` (line 335) / `min-width:60px` (line 556); switchTab sets active (line 1812) with no scrollIntoView on bn.
- **Impact:** Minor wayfinding nit: on phones the highlighted bottom-nav item for later tabs can be scrolled out of view, so users lose the 'you are here' cue until they scroll the bar.
- **Fix hint:** In switchTab, after setting the active bottom-nav button call `bn.scrollIntoView({inline:'center',block:'nearest'})` (guarded). Additive JS, no storage change.

## [P3] payroll-L08 — MOBILE_CSS forces slip KPI tiles to 2-up at 360px, overriding the module's intended 1-column layout
- **Module/area:** payroll | **Confidence:** high | **Combo:** 360xMobile
- **Location:** payroll.html — #pane-slips .stiles (line 354) + @media(max-width:360px) 1fr (line 683); shell MOBILE_CSS (index.html line 6325).
- **Defect:** The module deliberately collapses the Salary-Slips payroll-summary KPI tiles to a single column at the narrowest phones (`@media(max-width:360px){#pane-slips .stiles{grid-template-columns:1fr}}`, line 683). But the shell payroll MOBILE_CSS sets `#pane-slips .stiles{grid-template-columns:repeat(2,1fr) !important}` (index.html line 6325); the `!important` beats the module's non-important 360px rule, so at 360xMobile the tiles stay 2-up (~160px each) instead of full-width. Not broken (two tiles do fit) but it defeats the author's intended single-column readout and can cramp longer tile values.
- **Evidence:** module `@media (max-width:360px){ ... #pane-slips .stiles{grid-template-columns:1fr} }` (lines 678-684); shell `html.bcc-mobile[data-mod="payroll"] #pane-slips .stiles{grid-template-columns:repeat(2,1fr) !important}` (index.html line 6325).
- **Impact:** Cosmetic: on the very narrowest phones the slip summary tiles are 2-up rather than the intended 1-up, slightly tighter than designed.
- **Fix hint:** If single-column at <=360 is desired, the shell rule should not use !important (or should add a <=360 override to 1fr). Low priority; harmless either way.
