## 3. C-07 — measured with the shipped comparator

faithfully and run with the current analyser against both product states.

### 3.1 Message contracts

| Side | Contracts | Resolved | Unresolved |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Anchor product (Gate 0) | 68 | 20 | 48 |
| Migrated product (HEAD) | 57 | 20 | **37** |

```
message-contract deltas: 0
```

The comparator is deliberately asymmetric, and that asymmetry is what makes this
result meaningful rather than lucky:

- a resolved contract may not be **added**, and may not be **removed** while it
  still has both senders and receivers — a paired contract is protected behaviour;
- removing a one-sided dead contract is allowed, because it reduces coupling;
- for unresolved contracts only **additions** count. The reduction from 48 to 37
  is an improvement and correctly produces no delta.

All 20 resolved contracts survive with their senders, receivers and payload
shapes intact. No new sender shape was introduced.
